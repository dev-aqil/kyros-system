# Brand - Kyros System

_Status: active_

## Visual language

- **Background:** warm cream, reserved for the working surface.
- **Foreground:** charcoal, for calm and highly legible operational information.
- **Primary accent:** protection green, used only for protected states and primary actions.
- **Warning:** muted rose, used only where coverage is incomplete or an action needs attention.
- **Typography:** editorial serif for the product name and key numbers; clean system sans-serif for controls and explanations.

## Voice

Clear, calm, and operational. Kyros says "protect payment budget" rather than options jargon. It never implies a trade or payment succeeded until the relevant network confirms it.
